        Account publishing
        
    These links point to your public pages; they do not log in or publish for you. Direct publishing needs a public HTTPS app, approved developer apps and a secure account-connection flow. Do not enter passwords or access tokens here.
    
      
    
      
    
        On-device drafts
        
    Unfinished part forms, your selected photo, the last completed identification and the active tab are kept in this browser only, scoped to the signed-in account. They survive a mobile tab switch or reload, are never uploaded, and are deleted on sign-out. A local draft is not an inventory record until you save it.
    
        
      
    
      
    
        Cloud backup
        
    A backup script and Windows Task Scheduler setup guide are included with the project. Cloud upload stays disabled until you configure a private S3-compatible bucket and credentials in the local .env file.
